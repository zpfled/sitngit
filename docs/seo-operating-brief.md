Sit & Git Portables SEO Operating Brief

Document roles: this brief provides strategy; the [performance scorecard](seo-performance-scorecard.md)
contains dated measurements. Working economics, launch expectations, competitor details,
and operational status elsewhere in this brief are assumptions/context, not refreshed
performance measurements. Check their dates and evidence before relying on them.
1. Business objective

SEO should produce qualified, profitable leads inside the core service territory, not maximize raw traffic, impressions, rankings, or geographic reach.

Primary business outcomes:

more one-time event bookings
more recurring construction/long-term accounts
stronger local authority in Vernon, Richland, and Crawford Counties
better credibility with builders, GCs, municipalities, and public works prospects
prepare the website to make self-service online booking a major competitive advantage

4. Positioning

Do not position Sit & Git as the cheapest provider.

Customers may initially switch because of price, but retention is driven by:

service quality
cleanliness
equipment quality
reliability
responsiveness

Primary positioning:

A modern, local portable-restroom company offering clean equipment, reliable service, responsive communication, and an easy customer experience.

Online pricing differentiation (rollout prepared 2026-10-16):

Customers can see calculated rental pricing before providing contact details, then continue to book or request confirmation. Some requests require confirmation; do not promise guaranteed availability.

Price should be discovered during quoting/booking, not used as the brand promise.

Rollout architecture (supersedes the earlier proposed same-domain API integration):

14. Decision rules for all future SEO work

For every recommendation:

First ask:
Will this likely generate more qualified leads, improve local authority, increase conversion, or strengthen trust with target buyers?

If not, deprioritize it.

Rate each recommendation by:

Business impact: high / medium / low
Effort: high / medium / low
Confidence: high / medium / low
Expected horizon: immediate / weeks / months
Primary metric affected

Prefer expected business value relative to effort, not simply quick wins.

Quick wins should be identified, but should not automatically outrank more valuable medium-term work.

Challenge Zach’s keyword assumptions when evidence suggests a better opportunity.

Never manufacture recommendations just to produce an audit.

If the existing implementation is already good, say so and leave it alone.
